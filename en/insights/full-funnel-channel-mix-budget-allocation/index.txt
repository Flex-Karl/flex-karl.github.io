2e:T6b1,MediaMixModel (September 19, 2026) argues the fixed percentage is the wrong tool entirely and replaces it with a six-step operating method. Set an economic ceiling from unit economics first, not last year's budget plus ten percent: with a $300 customer lifetime value and a required 3:1 LTV-to-CAC ratio, your blended CAC ceiling is $100, and total paid spend must stop where blended CAC crosses it. 30:T4ce,QRY's four signals, tracked on a 30-day cadence, are the dashboard for the system as a whole rather than any single channel.
